_You have been ordained a priest of the Church. The Church is universal across the Empire, united in its worship of the Authority. While bishops and cardinals bicker over political and theological issues, local parishes celebrate thousands of different saints, martyrs and traditions._

##### Last updated October 2026.

<ins>Starting Equipment</ins><br>
A *Holy Book* (use as mace), *Monk Habits* (required to use your abilities), as well as your choice of two of the following:
- _Barrel of Wine_ that you can strangely carry (cumbersome, refilled when {% include tooltip.html term="tip_carousing" %})
- _Bestiary_ on evil spirits and how to banish them
- _Crossbow_ and six {% include tooltip.html term="tip_silverweapon" %} _Bolts_ (refilled when Carousing)
- *[Discipline Whip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discipline_(instrument_of_penance))* (1D4 nonlethal damage)
- _[Follower](https://coinsandscrolls.blogspot.com/2017/06/osr-table-of-camp-followers.html)_
- _[Thurible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thurible)_ (burn to {% include tooltip.html term="tip_resting" %} undisturbed, refilled when Carousing)

<ins>Starting Skills</ins><br>
The *Holy Book*, and one of the following additional {% include tooltip.html term="tip_skill" %}\: *Court Intrigue*, *Dead Languages*, *Inquisition*, *Luxury*, or *Preaching*.

## The Church
The Church is a powerful sprawling institution with rigid canon laws. Depending on how orthodox the sacraments you choose are, your standing within the institution will rise or fall. Each time you take a level in this class, roll a D4 on the **Story Beats** table, adding your _Standing Score_ to the result. At the referee's discretion, certain game events can also affect your standing.

**Story Beats**<br>
<details markdown="1">
<summary>-3. <ins>A split diocese!</ins></summary>
Your unorthodox ideas have disciples in the parish. The town is now split in two religious factions: Your followers, and the orthodoxy. Roll when you encounter a NPC to know which side they stand with.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>-2. <ins>Accused of heresy!</ins></summary>
The Archbishop has sent the inquisition after you. The referee will add a rival party to the encounter tables of your next adventure.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>-1. <ins>Banished!</ins></summary>
Fearing retribution from the Church, the populace has banished your from town!
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>0. <ins>A test of loyalty.</ins></summary>
The Archbishop is testing your loyalty by demanding you deal with local heretics. The referee will give you a target in your next adventure. Increase or decrease your _Status_ depending on how you deal with it.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>1. <ins>A touched soul.</ins></summary>
One of the town's citizens has become smitten by your words, you gain a new _[Follower](https://coinsandscrolls.blogspot.com/2017/06/osr-table-of-camp-followers.html)_.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>2. <ins>A small gift for your mission.</ins></summary>
The Church is providing you with 4 vials of Holy Water, use them well.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>3. <ins>An inspired artist.</ins></summary>
A local artist has painted a Saint’s Icon for you to carry. Roll on the [Failed Medieval Career](https://tenfootpolemic.blogspot.com/2014/01/200-failed-medieval-careers.html) table. You can add 1D6 to your rolls related to this career, and +1 to your prayers targeting people of this craft.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>4. <ins>A recommended letter.</ins></summary>
The Archbishop has sent you an official letter of recommendation. It can be traded anywhere in the Empire for a favour, at the referee's discretion.
</details>
