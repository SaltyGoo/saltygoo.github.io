Glitzle Candy dissolves in water with a bubbly fizz that lingers for 20 minutes. It can be harvested from **glitzle fruits** commonly grown in **sound gardens**.

#### Effect
One single candy makes you {% include tooltip.html term="tip_status_intoxicated" %} until you {% include tooltip.html term="tip_resting" %}, as if you had consumed a large quantity of alcohol (without the bloat, as the dryads would remind you). Each additional candy prevents you from resting and being {% include tooltip.html term="tip_status_exhausted" %} for 24 hours.
