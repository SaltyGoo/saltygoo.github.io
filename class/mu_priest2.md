_You have been ordained a priest of the Church. The Church is universal across the Empire, united in its worship of the Authority. While bishops and cardinals bicker over political and theological issues, local parishes celebrate thousands of different saints, martyrs and traditions._

##### Last updated October 2026.

<ins>Starting Equipment</ins><br>
A *Holy Book* (use as mace), *Monk Habits* (required to use your abilities), as well as your choice of two of the following:
- _Barrel of Wine_ that you can strangely carry (cumbersome, refilled when {% include tooltip.html term="tip_carousing" %})
- _Bestiary_ on evil spirits and how to banish them
- _Crossbow_ and six {% include tooltip.html term="tip_silverweapon" %} _Bolts_ (refilled when Carousing)
- *[Discipline Whip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discipline_(instrument_of_penance))* (1D4 nonlethal damage)
- _[Follower](https://coinsandscrolls.blogspot.com/2017/06/osr-table-of-camp-followers.html)_
- _[Thurible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thurible)_ (burn to {% include tooltip.html term="tip_resting" %} undisturbed, refilled when Carousing)

<ins>Starting Skills</ins><br>
The *Holy Book*, and one of the following additional {% include tooltip.html term="tip_skill" %}\: *Court Intrigue*, *Dead Languages*, *Inquisition*, *Luxury*, or *Preaching*.

## The Church
The Church is a powerful sprawling institution with rigid canon laws. Depending on how orthodox the sacraments you choose are, your standing within the institution will rise or fall. Each time you take a level in this class, roll a D6 on the Story Beats table, adding your _Standing Score_ to the result. At the referee's discretion, certain game events can also affect your standing.

**Story Beats**<br>
<details markdown="1">
<summary>-3. <ins>Banished!</ins></summary>
Fearing retribution from the Church, the populace has banished your from town!</details>
<details markdown="1">
<summary>-2. <ins>Accused of heresy!</ins></summary>
The Archbishop has sent the inquisition after you. The referee will add a rival party to the encounter tables of your next adventure.</details>
